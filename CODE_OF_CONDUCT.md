# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and keep discussions on topic. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language, and publishing other people's private information (including data from production logs) are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If someone behaves in a way that breaks this code, contact the maintainer, [@talikbaku](https://github.com/talikbaku), privately: open a [private report](https://github.com/talikbaku/api-dead-ends/security/advisories/new) with "Code of conduct" in the title (it is visible only to the maintainer), or use GitHub's [Report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and block people who break this code, temporarily or permanently. Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
